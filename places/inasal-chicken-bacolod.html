Inasal Chicken Bacolod

Inasal Chicken Bacolod is a solid pick when you want a full, satisfying meal without breaking the bank. The smoky grilled chicken paired with garlic rice is filling enough to power through an afternoon of classes, and it's conveniently located inside SM City Manila. Great option for group meals since it's easy to order a few extra sides to share.

Detail Information
Address SM City Manila, Low Ground, Main Building
Hours Follows SM City Manila mall hours
Price Range ₱150 – ₱250 per meal
Best Time to Visit Weekday afternoons to avoid mall crowds
