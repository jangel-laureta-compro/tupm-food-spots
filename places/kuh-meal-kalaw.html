Kuh Meal Kalaw

Kuh Meal Kalaw is my go-to spot when I need a quick, filling meal between classes without spending too much. It's just a short walk from campus, making it perfect for a lunch break. Portions are generous for the price, and the staff are quick, so you're rarely stuck waiting during the lunch rush.

Detail Information
Address Parkview Plaza, Kalaw, Manila
Hours Mon–Sat 9:00 AM–9:00 PM, Sun 11:30 AM–8:30 PM
Price Range ₱80 – ₱150 per meal
Best Time to Visit Before 11:30 AM to avoid the lunch rush
