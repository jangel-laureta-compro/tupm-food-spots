Daddy's Sisig

Daddy's Sisig is the spot for when you're craving something rich and savory after a long day of classes. Their sisig comes out sizzling hot on a cast iron plate, packed with flavor and just the right amount of crispiness. Best enjoyed with a cold drink and maybe a friend or two to split a plate with.

Detail Information
Address 720 Mercedes Street, Ermita, Manila
Landmark Behind San Marcelino St., near Adamson University High School
Price Range ₱120 – ₱200 per plate
Best Time to Visit Evenings, for the full sizzling experience
